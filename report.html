01Exploratory Data Analysis

The spec requires an EDA before fixing the recipe-selection policy: if the corpus offers a reliable, comparable signal (popularity, views, likes), it may replace the lowest-stable-ID baseline. The EDA measured every candidate signal's coverage and distinct-value spread across all 49 records.

Metadata coverage per field (usable-signal candidates)

A signal is usable only at ≥ 80% coverage and ≥ 2 distinct values. Rating (2%) and time (47%) both fail decisively.

Total-time distribution (minutes, n=23)

Good spread across quick and slow dishes — time-constraint questions have real support. 27/49 unknowns are excluded from hard matches, never filled in.

Servings coverage

59% of records declare servings — useful for yield questions, not a ranking signal.

EDA decision

Verdict: stable_id_baseline

Rating coverage 0.02 (1 record, 1 distinct value) and time coverage 0.47 both fail the ≥ 0.8 / ≥ 2 criteria. No reliable, comparable signal exists in this corpus, therefore the lowest stable recipe ID remains the deterministic selection strategy after relevance and hard-constraint filtering.

Any future switch must be versioned, documented in ADR-001 and covered by deterministic tests — exactly as spec §4.2 and RET-08 require.

03What the Corpus Gives the Project

Concrete, measured properties the retrieval / grounding / eval phases can build on.

Selection-category distribution

Vegetarian and desserts dominate — the two categories that drive diet-constraint and discovery eval cases.

Most common normalized ingredients

Ingredient lookups and required/excluded-ingredient filters have dense support: salt appears in 17/49 recipes, water in 8, sugar in 7+2 variants. The synonym map for filters will be built on this frequency data.

Vegetarian answers ready for the demo question

"What's a vegetarian dinner I can make in under 30 minutes?" — the assignment's own example — has 4 grounded answers in this corpus.

Recipe complexity spread

Ingredients per recipe: min 2 / median 9 / max 41. Steps: min 1 / median 7 / max 31. The spread exercises chunking and grounding at both extremes.
